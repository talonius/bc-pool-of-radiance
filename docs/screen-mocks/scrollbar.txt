The scroll bar (scrollbar.png, scrollbar-edge.png)

A list longer than its box shows a scroll bar in the box's right border.
The bar replaces the border's edge between the box's corners (or the joints
where another box meets that edge). When the list fits the box there is no
bar and the border is drawn as usual.

Load/Save Game, a full-screen box: the bar is column 39, rows 1 to 22,
between the corner boxes on rows 0 and 23.

The Buy screen's list box: the bar is column 39, rows 3 to 15, between the
joints on rows 2 and 16. Its old bar inside the box (column 37, '*' and

COLORS

Light comes from the upper left, as on the gold corner boxes: lit edges are
on the top and left, shadows on the bottom and right. Three greys and black:

    W  white        EGA White (15)
    g  grey         EGA Light Gray (7)
    d  dark grey    EGA Dark Gray (8)
    .  black        EGA Black (0)

THE KNOB'S SIZE AND PLACE

The arrows take the bar's first and last rows; the knob moves in the rows
between them (the track rows). Its length and position come from
MenuCore.ScrollThumb, as the Buy screen's bar does now: the share of the
track the knob covers is the share of the list shown, at least one row; it
touches the up arrow only at the list's start and the down arrow only at
its end.

Load/Save shows 7 of up to 11 slots on 20 track rows: a knob of 13 rows.

The grip goes on the knob's middle cell: cell size div 2, counting from 0
(the lower of the two middle cells when the size is even). A knob of 1 or 2
cells has no grip; 1 cell uses the one-cell picture.


THE ART

The pieces are made by the Data Builder and stored in a DAX file, with the
other data it builds, so the game draws finished cells and recolors
nothing while it runs:

    up arrow, down arrow    drawn from the pictures above (symbol 256's
                            and 258's arrows redrawn, their combat grey
                            gone)
    track                   the checkerboard above
    knob top, middle,       the pictures above
      bottom, one cell
    grip                    symbol 276 recolored as above

The game draws them through the 8x8 symbol path (Sym8x8) at the border's
cells. Anything that redraws the border under a list redraws the bar after
it.
