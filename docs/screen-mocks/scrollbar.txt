The scroll bar (scrollbar.png, scrollbar-edge.png)

A list longer than its box shows a scroll bar in the box's right border.
The bar replaces the border's edge between the box's corners (or the joints
where another box meets that edge). When the list fits the box there is no
bar and the border is drawn as usual.

Load/Save Game, a full-screen box: the bar is column 39, rows 1 to 22,
between the corner boxes on rows 0 and 23.

COLORS

Light comes from the upper left, as on the gold corner boxes: lit edges are
on the top and left, shadows on the bottom and right. Three greys and black:

    W  white        EGA White (15)
    g  grey         EGA Light Gray (7)
    d  dark grey    EGA Dark Gray (8)
    .  black        EGA Black (0)

THE KNOB'S SIZE AND PLACE

The arrows take the bar's first and last rows; the knob moves in the rows
between them (the track rows). Its length and position come from
Border.ScrollThumb: the share of the track the knob covers is the share
of the list shown, rounded to the nearest row, at least one row and, while
the list is longer than the box, at least one row short of the track; it
touches the up arrow only at the list's start and the down arrow only at
its end. (ScrollThumb is given the track's length apart from the list's,
since the arrows take two of the bar's rows and an entry may be several
rows, as a Load/Save slot is three.)

Load/Save shows 7 of up to 11 slots on 20 track rows: a knob of 13 rows.

The grip goes on the knob's middle cell: cell size div 2, counting from 0
(the lower of the two middle cells when the size is even). A knob of 1 or 2
cells has no grip; 1 cell uses the one-cell picture.


THE ART

The pieces are made by the Data Builder and stored in the merged 8X8D.DAX
as block 250, with the other data it builds, so the game draws finished
cells, recolors nothing while it runs, and the cells can be looked at and
changed in the archive (scripts/mock-render/glyphs.py --dax 8X8D.DAX shows
them). src/barart.pas holds the pictures; block 250 is a picture as symbol
block 202 is, 202's header with 8 frames:

    up arrow, down arrow    drawn from the pictures above (symbol 256's
                            and 258's arrows redrawn, their combat grey
                            gone)
    track                   the checkerboard above
    knob top, middle,       the pictures above
      bottom, one cell
    grip                    symbol 276 recolored as above

    symbol  287 up arrow      288 down arrow   289 track
            290 knob top      291 knob middle  292 knob bottom
            293 knob, one cell                 294 grip

The game loads them as symbol set 5 (Sym8x8.LoadScrollBar, after the Data
Builder has run) and draws them through the 8x8 symbol path (Put8x8Sym) at
the border's cells. Block ids 250 to 252 are kept for data the port adds:
no shipped archive uses them, and the Data Builder gives no colliding block
one. The per-disk archives, which the game reads when the merged one could
not be built, have no block 250; then there are no cells
(Sym8x8.HasScrollBar is False) and the bar must be drawn without them. Anything that redraws the border under a list redraws the bar after
it.
