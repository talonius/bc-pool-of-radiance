If the item isn't usable by the current PC, the new values 
would become dashes ("--")
CHAR: and POOL: would be in WHITE.
2,014 GP / 15,102 GP (character and party gold) would be in YELLOW.

the list to the top of the displayed items.

Scrolling (agreed after the mock):
The list scrolls a line at a time: UP/DOWN past the top or bottom row
move the list one line, and stop at the ends of the list. PAGE UP and
PAGE DOWN still move a page. There is no NEXT or PREV.
The scroll bar in column 37 is shown only when the list is longer than
the 13 rows. The thumb ("*", WHITE) is sized to the share of the list
shown, at least one row, and follows the top of the visible window; the
track ("-") is DARK GRAY.
Ammunition: on a bow with matching arrows readied, NEW DMG is the bow's
damage with the arrows; with none readied it is the bow's own damage.
